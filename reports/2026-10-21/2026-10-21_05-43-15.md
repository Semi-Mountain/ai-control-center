### 主题 2｜上下文即架构：压缩失败为什么必须「按证据组」而非「按相似度」处理

- **arXiv:2607.21503** — *Agentic Context Management: Solving Agent Memory and Cost by Treating Them as Lifecycle and Architecture Problems*（2026-07-23）  
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21503  
  核心引用：> “Production AI agents' failures are less often due to an inability to reason well and more often because they cannot manage what is in their reasoning context … treating it as a lifecycle, not merely a store.”  
  机制：会话历史、工具定义、工具输出持续累积，token 成本随之上升；压缩到预算内而不丢关键内容，本身是架构问题。对应爸爸的组件：`context_compressor` 的 `protect_last_n` / `threshold` / `target_ratio` 与「无摘要丢轮次」的降级路径。
- **arXiv:2609.35832** — *When Should LLMs Trust Their Own Revisions? A Risk-Aware Study of Intrinsic Self-Correction*（2026-09-23）  
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35832  
  核心引用：> “A second pass can recover mistakes, but it can also overturn answers that were already correct … Aggregate accuracy can conceal substantially different revision behavior.”  
  机制：自修订是**有损**操作；应比较「保留初答 / 全盘接受修订 / 选择性触发」三种运行时策略，而非无脑二次生成。对应：07:00 自评的 debate/commit-first 模式。
- **arXiv:2610.23266** — *Beyond Marginal Influence: Interaction-Aware Memory Selection for LLM Agents*（2026-10-16）  
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23266  
  核心引用：> “Such rankings cannot identify sufficient subsets under redundant or jointly necessary evidence … cost-ordered JointCore recovers a minimum-cost sufficient set in 100.0% of cases, reduces subset evaluations from 30.00 to 11.13 (62.9%), and achieves 87.8% active-memory savings.”  
  机制：**边际相关性排名不足以判「已充分覆盖」**，必须按证据组做联合删除。对应：G5 增量去重不应只做标题相似度 diff。
