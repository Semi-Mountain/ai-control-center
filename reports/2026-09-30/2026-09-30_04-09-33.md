## 🔍 三层挖深

**机制层**:
- Reflective赢的核心场景:诊断说不出具体值(certified brand/exact scaling/cuisine-specific替换)
- Reflective**不赢**的场景:诊断已命名熟悉概念(meringue+baking powder)、LLM能从substrate自己重算

**实现层**(Schema v0.1):
```json
{"recovery_feedback": {
  "type": "RECOVERY_GUIDANCE",
  "suggestions": [
    {"type": "USE_SPECIFIC_BRAND", "parameters": {"with_specific_brand": "Bob's Red Mill Certified GF"}}
  ]
}}
```
