## 🔍 三个核心信息源（挖深铁律：≥3 信息源 + 挖3层：现象→机制→实现）

### 1. **Pinned and Still Unstable: Within-Judge Verdict Variance and the Noise Floor of LLM-as-Judge Leaderboards** (arXiv:2609.33044v2, 2026-09)
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33044v2
- **核心引用** (原文):
  > "Modern LLM evaluation assumes that pinning a judge to a fixed model version and decoding at temperature zero yields reproducible verdicts. We show this assumption fails as a property of how LLM-as-Judge is operationalized on cloud serving infrastructure, not of any particular model family."
  > "per-item flip rates of roughly 5% on average and about **40% on the close-call items that decide leaderboard margins**... **Kendall's tau of 0.42-0.64** between Gemini and Sonnet judges on Arena-Hard."
  > "We propose a minimal, low-cost reporting protocol: **several judge re-runs, published stability profiles and adjacency intervals**, and results under at least two judges from different families."
- **机制层**: 即使 pin model + temp=0，verdict 仍 flip —— 不是 model 问题，是云服务侧的不稳定性。**5% 平均 flip / 40% 临界项 flip** + **judge 间 Kendall τ ∈ [0.42, 0.64]** 揭示 absolute-difference comparison 完全失效：score 差 0.05 看似"接近"，实际可能是 judge 漂移噪声。
- **实现细节**: 报告协议 = ① 多次重跑 ② stability profile (waver fraction + conditional intensity) ③ adjacency intervals ④ 至少 2 个不同 family 的 judge。
- **wowzai 映射**:
  - trace_cig_align v0.2 的 `abs(sr/10 - cig_confidence)` → **必须升级到 rank-based** (Spearman ρ / Kendall τ)
  - self_rewarder v0.6 的 `_derive_patch_verdicts` 加一个 **flip_rate 字段**：同一 note 重跑 3 次 verdict 有多少 p-value flip → 直接对照论文 5%/40% 阈值报警
  - 07:00 报告默认带 "Verdict stability: 8/15 supported stable, 4/15 wavered (5% baseline)"

### 2. **VERA: Verdict-Conditioned Reliability for Adaptive LLM Judges** (arXiv:2610.05452v1, 2026-10) ⭐ 新鲜
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05452v1
- **核心引用** (原文):
  > "existing approaches often rely on **output-level confidence, which can be overconfident and poorly aligned with judgment correctness**. We propose VERA, a **VErdict-conditioned Reliability Axis** that estimates reliability from hidden activations by distinguishing correct from incorrect judgments within each predicted-verdict group."
- **机制层**: output-level confidence（LLM 自己说「我 80% 确信」）**不可信**——过 confidence。VERA 把 reliability 拆解为 verdict-conditioned：每个 verdict (supported/refuted/abstained/drifted) 内部单独算 reliability axis，让「判为 supported」本身有可信度梯度。这跟 wowzai v0.6 `_derive_patch_verdicts` 高度对位——verdict 应当 first-class 同时带 reliability。
- **实现细节**: VERA 从 hidden activations 而不是 output logits 取 reliability → 说明 raw score (per_principle_mean) 不能直接当 verdict 用，必须经过一层 **reliability axis** 投影。
- **wowzai 映射**:
  - v0.6 patch_verdicts 输出 → 加 `verdict_reliability: float (0-1)` 字段，对每 verdict 组内单独 calibrate
  - trace_cig_align v0.3 不光比对 sr_score vs cig_confidence，还要比对 **sr_verdict vs cig_verdict** 的 4×4 confusion matrix
  - 借鉴 VERA 的「verdict group 内 calibrate」思路 → 在 abstained first-class 之外加 **verdict-band 可信度** (这跟 HackProbe 2609.04665 的 4 维 reward hacking 检测互补)

### 3. **RubricEval: A Rubric-Level Meta-Evaluation Benchmark for LLM Judges in Instruction Following** (arXiv:2603.25133v1, 2026-03, abstract verified)
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25133v1
- **核心引用** (原文):
  > "even GPT-4o, a widely adopted judge in instruction-following benchmarks, achieves only **55.97% on Hard subset**. Considering evaluation paradigm, **rubric-level evaluation outperforms checklist-level**, explicit reasoning improves accuracy, and both together reduce inter-judge variance."
- **机制层**: rubric-level (即 wowzai 的 patch_verdicts) 比 checklist-level (单纯 binary yes/no) 准确得多；explicit reasoning (LCB / confidence 区间) 显著降方差。**这给 03 段 patch_verdicts 设计提供了 paper-validated 的实证背书**。
- **实现细节**: Easy/Hard subsets split + GPT-4o Hard 55.97% → 即使最强 judge 在 hard 段也只能 56% 准确率，**说明 wowzai self_rewarder 不能被 100% 信任，必须配 abstained first-class**。
- **wowzai 映射**:
  - 03 段已经在 v0.6 加了 abstained first-class（21 个 verdict 里 11 个 abstained）→ 跟 RubricEval 实证吻合
  - 04 段落地时建议**保留 abstained 主导**——因为当前 16 个 principle 里 hard 类多，58% abstained 是"诚实不确定"而非"评估失败"
  - 05 段 SIGMA review 应按 "verdict distribution 健康度" 检测: abstained 占比 >60% 报警（说明题目太 hard，需降难度）

### 4. **Feed the Panel Dimensions, Not Verdicts: Rubric-Decomposed Fusion** (arXiv:2609.27110v1, 2026-09) ⭐ 强烈对位
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27110v1
- **核心引用** (原文):
  > "the dimension scores measure what their labels claim: with the overall human score partialled out, a **dimension prompt carries more attribute-specific information than the holistic prompt in 28 of 30 model-attribute cells**... they beat the best single VLM in all ten three-family panels on EVA (+0.07 Spearman rho for the strongest trio...)"
- **机制层**: panel of holistic judges **从不显著 beat 最佳单 judge**；但**把 holistic 拆成多维 rubric 后 fuse** → 显著提升。**Spearman ρ +0.07** vs panel mean +0.118.
- **实现细节**: 关键不是换 judge，是**换输入维度**。给每个 judge 多维 rubric prompts (per-dimension) 而不是 holistic prompt，再 fuse。
- **wowzai 映射**:
  - wowzai self_rewarder 当前是 holistic prompt 给 1 个 overall_mean → 应**升级为 per_principle P0..P15 rubric**（已经在做，但 fusion 是简单 mean）→ 加 **per-principle weighted Spearman fusion** 取代 `mean(L1) + mean(L2)`
  - trace_cig_align 不应比对 overall_mean，而应比对 **per_principle rank**: 把 16 个 P0..P15 各自 rank，再算 correlation

### 5. **Reliability of LLM Judges for Evaluating Entity Alignment** (arXiv:2610.09554v1, 2026-10) ⭐ 完美对位 trace_cig_align
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09554v1
- **机制层**: EA (entity alignment) 本质就是 trace_cig_align 的核心任务 (segment ↔ cig node ↔ patch verdict 三方对齐)。论文专门研究 LLM judge 做这种 cross-source 对齐任务的 reliability。
- **wowzai 映射**: 把 trace_cig_align 当成 "EA-like 任务" 看待，借鉴其 perturbation bias diagnostics → 给 cig/trace 加扰动版本，看 align 结果是否 stable → 加 **alignment_stability** 字段

### 6. **Conditional Accuracy Profiles: Diagnosing LLM Judges across Deployment Conditions** (arXiv:2610.09229v1, 2026-10) ⭐
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09229v1
- **核心引用** (原文 partial): "We introduce Conditional Accuracy Profiling (CAP), a **post-hoc diagnostic framework that decomposes pairwise LLM-judge accuracy into eight conditions** organized into **content sensitivity, robustness, and rationale quality**."

## 🔬 v0.2.1 实证发现（04 段真跑数据 · 新发现 root cause）

刚跑了 trace_cig_align v0.2.1 真数据，**alignment_rate = 0% 仍未解**：

```json
    "total_segments": 4,

    "total_drifted": 0,
    "total_abstained": 1,
    "unresolved_aliases": ["P01_selfreward_lcb"],
    "alignment_rate": 0.0
  }
}
```

**真 root cause（不是 Problem D band）**：
- cig v0.2 schema 实际把 patches 放在 `created_segments[].patches_to_cig[].node_id`
- 而 trace_cig_align v0.2.1 期待 `cig.graph.propagation_graph.relations`（G_f schema）
- → 03 段 cig 升级到 v0.2 **没有把 patches 移到 G_f relations 字段** → cig_edges = []
- → 一切 patch 变 abstained

**升级方案 → v0.3**：
1. **schema 桥接**: `index_propagation_edges` 加 fallback——如果 `graph.propagation_graph` 空，从 `created_segments[].patches_to_cig` 自动生成 relations: `{from: parent_patch_or_seg, to: patch, confidence: patches_to_cig.confidence || 0.5, type: "implements"}`
2. **rank-based alignment**: `_band_for_score` + `_band_for_confidence` 后加 `_rank_alignment(sr_scores, cig_confs)`，用 `1 - spearmanr(sr_ranks, cig_ranks).correlation` 替代 abs(delta)
3. **per-condition breakdown**: 输出 `alignment_by_alias / by_verdict / by_orphan / by_drift` 四维
4. **stability_axis**: 加 alignment_stability 字段（2609.33044 思路），cross-run 三次取 std
5. **rationale_score**: 加 0-1 rationale quality（2610.09229 CAP 借鉴）

**预期**: 改 schema 桥接后 alignment_rate 0% → ≥80%（4 类基本盘已修）+ rank-based 替代 absolute 后 drift 检测更准。
